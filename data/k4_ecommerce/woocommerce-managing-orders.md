Orders are the center of activity in your store. Understanding how to manage them effectively is key to keeping your business running smoothly.

This documentation is a guide to managing orders in WooCommerce: order statuses, the Orders overview screen, editing orders (view/update items and totals), manually creating orders, adding line items or fees, using order notes to communicate with customers, paying for orders, and removing personal data to comply with privacy regulations.
