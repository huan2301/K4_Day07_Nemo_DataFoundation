Order fulfillment is the workflow of processing, managing, and shipping customer orders from your Shopify store. This includes setting up shipping configuration, managing incoming orders, and getting packages to customers.

You can manage orders from the Orders page in your Shopify admin. This includes capturing payments, reviewing order details, printing shipping documents, preparing items for fulfillment, issuing refunds, or canceling orders when needed.

You can fulfill orders yourself, use a fulfillment service, or combine methods. After preparing an order, you fulfill it by picking, packing, and shipping items to your customer. Shopify also provides guidance on buying/printing shipping labels and managing shipping carriers.
