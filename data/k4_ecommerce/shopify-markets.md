Markets lets you manage how different customers experience your store based on location, customer group, retail location, or sales channel. You can tailor experiences with customizations including currency, language, pricing, and theme content.

The Markets documentation explains how to configure market-specific settings and mentions return/refund-related settings and shipping options by market. It also links to setup for shipping options and backup regions.
