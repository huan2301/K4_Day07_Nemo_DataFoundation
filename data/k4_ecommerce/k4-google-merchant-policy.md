This policy applies to Shopping ads and local inventory ads.

Google doesn’t want customers to feel misled by the content promoted in your offers, and that means being upfront, honest, and providing them with the information that they need to make informed decisions.

The page lists disallowed practices such as offers that prompt customers to commit without providing all relevant information, misrepresenting products or business identity, omission of return and refund policy, and other unacceptable business practices. It also gives examples and best practices (deliver what customers paid for, describe your business, avoid misleading use of other brands, disclose partnerships).

Actions for violations: accounts may be suspended; remedies include submitting an appeal and correcting product data. (Excerpted from the official Google Merchant Center support page.)
